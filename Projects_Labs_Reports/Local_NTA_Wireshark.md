## <span style="color:#0a75ad">Executive Summary</span>
<blockquote><p>On July 27, 2026, network monitoring identified unexpected outbound TCP connections on
destination port 8009 from workstation **192.168.1.224** to an unmanaged endpoint
**192.168.1.217**. Initial packet inspection flagged potential Apache JServ Protocol (AJP13)
anomalies. Endpoint artifact correlation and SSDP packet inspection confirmed the traffic was
standard Google Cast / DIAL service auto-discovery generated by local Chromium-based
processes **(chrome.exe, msedge.exe, brave.exe, steamwebhelper.exe)**. The activity is
classified as **Benign (False Positive)** with no indicators of compromise (IoCs) detected within
the scoped capture.
</p></blockquote>
